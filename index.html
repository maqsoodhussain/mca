About the MCA Program

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Kashmir University equips students with advanced skills in programming, software development, and emerging technologies.

The program blends theory with real-world practice through labs, projects, and internships — preparing graduates to excel in academia, research, and the IT industry.

Program Highlights

  • Updated syllabus (2025 batch) aligned with NEP guidelines
  • Expert faculty with strong academic & industry experience
  • Hands-on projects, research opportunities & internships
  • Strong placement support & career guidance

How We Contribute on GitHub

Our community works together to improve projects by sharing code, fixing issues, and managing resources on GitHub. Whether you're new or experienced, this is a great way to build your portfolio and learn by doing! 📚🛠️

Free Canva Premium & Figma Access

As a contributor, you get free access to Canva Premium and Figma — powerful design tools to create stunning visuals for your projects, portfolios, resumes, and more! 🎨✨ Just share your GitHub profile link and email to get access.
